# Instructions & Preferences

## Communication & Explanations
- **Clear & Direct:** Explain concepts clearly and thoroughly without being chaotic or overwhelming.
- **No Unnecessary Analogies:** Avoid forced analogies or fluff; explain things as they are.
- **Structured:** Group information logically so it's easy to digest at a glance.

## Coding Style
- **KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid):** Prefer simple, predictable, and intelligible implementations over clever or over-engineered solutions.
- **Minimal Boilerplate:** Avoid excessive boilerplate while maintaining full functionality, strong typing, and good aesthetics.
- **Clean & Readable:** Code should be self-documenting, formatted consistently, and easy to follow.
